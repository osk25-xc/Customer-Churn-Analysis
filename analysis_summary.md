\# Analysis Summary



\## Objective



The objective of this project is to analyze customer churn patterns and identify factors that may influence customer retention.



\## Tools Used



\- Python

\- Pandas

\- NumPy

\- Matplotlib

\- Seaborn

\- Jupyter Notebook



\## Analysis Performed



\- Data cleaning and preprocessing

\- Exploratory data analysis

\- Churn distribution analysis

\- Customer segment comparison

\- Visualization of churn patterns

\- Business-focused interpretation of results



\## Business Value



The analysis can help businesses identify customers who may be at higher risk of churn and support targeted customer retention strategies.
