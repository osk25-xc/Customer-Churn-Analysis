\# Customer Churn Insights



\## Analysis Summary



This project analyzes customer churn patterns using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.



\## Key Insights



\- Examined overall customer churn distribution.

\- Compared churn behavior across customer segments.

\- Analyzed important factors associated with customer churn.

\- Used visualizations to identify patterns in customer behavior.



\## Business Recommendations



\- Identify high-risk customers early.

\- Improve customer engagement and retention strategies.

\- Create targeted offers for customers showing signs of disengagement.

\- Monitor customer segments with consistently higher churn.
